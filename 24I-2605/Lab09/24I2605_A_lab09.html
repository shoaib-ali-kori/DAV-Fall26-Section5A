Data Analysis and Visualization — Lab Manual 9

Hierarchical, network and geospatial visualizations using D3.js

Task 1: Treemap Task 2: Dendrogram Task 3: Flight Network Task 4: Population Map Task 5: Pakistan Cities Task 6: Combined Map

Task 1: Course Enrollment Treemap

Each course rectangle represents student enrollment. Courses are grouped by department, and hovering shows course details.

Larger rectangles mean more students. Department colors are shown in the legend.

Task 2: University Structure Dendrogram

A horizontal tree of the university, departments and courses. Click a department node or label to collapse or expand its courses.

University (root) Department Course

Task 3: Flight Route Network

Cities are nodes, and flight routes are links. Link thickness shows weekly flights; node size shows the number of routes connected to that city. Drag nodes to rearrange the network.

Hover over a city to highlight its routes and fade unrelated routes. Drag a city to reposition it.

Task 4: World Population Choropleth

Countries are colored by population in millions. The population CSV is joined to country features by ISO-3 country code. Countries without a matching value are light gray.

Task 5: Pakistan Cities Point Map

Circle area represents city population. Only cities with population above 1 million are labelled; hover over any city to see its full details.

City location (circle area represents population)

Task 6: Combined Spatial Encoding Map

The map combines city location, population, hub status and weekly flight routes. A hub has four or more routes. Hover over a city to highlight its routes and dim the others.

Hub: 4+ routes Not a hub: fewer than 4 routes Circle area = population Line width = weekly flights

Routes are drawn first so the city circles remain visible on top.
